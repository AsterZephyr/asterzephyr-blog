Proposes an adaptive budget allocation strategy for real-time bidding systems that dynamically adjusts bid prices based on predicted conversion rates, remaining budget constraints, and intra-day traffic patterns. The approach combines a three-level budget hierarchy with K-factor pacing and slow-start warm-up, outperforming fixed-rate baselines on both spend efficiency and conversion volume across multiple ad exchanges.
