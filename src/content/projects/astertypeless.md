A macOS app that turns voice into text and drops it right where the cursor is. The pipeline runs ASR transcription through LLM polishing in under 2 seconds with a self-hosted model. Built with SwiftUI and Accessibility APIs, supports multiple ASR/LLM providers including self-hosted options. No app switching, no copy-paste.
